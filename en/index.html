🚬 Smoking: Slow Suicide

Real Consequences of Smoking

Smoking is one of the leading causes of preventable mortality worldwide. Each cigarette smoked shortens life by 11 minutes. Nicotine causes addiction comparable to heroin.

Smoking Facts:

  • Smoking causes 90% of lung cancer cases
  • Smokers have 2-4 times higher risk of coronary heart disease
  • Smoking accelerates skin aging by 10-20 years
  • Secondhand smoke kills 1.2 million people annually
  • Tobacco smoke contains over 7000 chemicals, 69 of which are carcinogenic

Alternatives and Help:

You can quit smoking at any age. Within 20 minutes after the last cigarette, blood pressure normalizes; within 48 hours, taste and smell recover; within a year, the risk of heart disease decreases by half.

Useful Resources:

  • Smoking cessation hotline: 1-800-QUIT-NOW (USA)
  • Free mobile apps for quitting smoking
  • Nicotine replacement therapy (patches, gums)
  • Cognitive-behavioral therapy for smokers

🍷 Alcohol: Legalized Poison

Truth About Alcohol

Alcohol is a psychoactive substance that causes addiction and leads to serious diseases. WHO recognizes alcohol as a carcinogen causing at least 7 types of cancer.

Alcohol Facts:

  • Alcohol kills 3 million people annually
  • Alcohol causes 5.3% of all deaths worldwide
  • Regular use shortens life by 10-15 years
  • Alcohol damages all organs, especially brain and liver
  • There is no "safe" dose of alcohol

Myths and Reality:

"A glass of red wine is good for the heart" - this myth has been refuted by modern research. Any amount of alcohol increases the risk of cardiovascular diseases, hypertension, and stroke.

How to Live Without Alcohol:

Sobriety is the natural state of a person. Social life, sports, hobbies, self-development provide much more pleasure than alcohol. Modern medicine offers effective methods for treating alcoholism.

Help and Support:

  • Alcoholics Anonymous: support groups worldwide
  • Addiction helpline: 1-800-662-HELP (USA)
  • Cognitive-behavioral therapy for addiction
  • Medical treatment under doctor's supervision

💉 Drugs: One-Way Ticket

Reality of Drug Addiction

Drugs destroy personality, physical health, and social connections. Addiction develops quickly, and treatment requires long time and effort. Drug addiction is not a lifestyle choice but a serious disease.

Drug Facts:

  • Average life expectancy of drug addicts: 5-10 years after starting use
  • Drug addiction increases suicide risk by 15-20 times
  • 90% of HIV-infected in Russia contracted through injection drugs
  • Drugs cause irreversible brain damage
  • Synthetic drugs can cause psychosis from first use

Why People Start Using Drugs:

Curiosity, peer pressure, desire to escape problems, search for new sensations - main reasons for first try. But the first try often becomes the last free decision in life.

Alternatives:

Real emotions, achievements, creativity, sports, relationships - all these provide much brighter and deeper experiences than drugs, and don't destroy life.

Where to Get Help:

  • Drug addiction hotline: 1-800-662-HELP
  • Free rehabilitation centers
  • Anonymous counseling with narcologists and psychologists
  • Replacement therapy programs under medical supervision

💔 Suicide: Not a Solution, But a Dead End

Suicide is Forever, Problems are Temporary

Suicidal thoughts are a sign of deep suffering, not weakness. In 90% of cases, suicidal behavior is associated with mental disorders that are treatable.

Suicide Facts:

  • Every 40 seconds, one person commits suicide worldwide
  • For every completed suicide attempt, there are 20 unfinished ones
  • 80% of people who committed suicide previously spoke about their intentions
  • After depression treatment, suicidal thoughts disappear in 70-80% of patients
  • Support from loved ones reduces suicide risk by 50%

Signs of Suicidal Behavior:

  • Talks about death, hopelessness, helplessness
  • Giving away valuable things, making a will
  • Sudden mood swings
  • Social isolation, saying goodbye to loved ones
  • Acquiring means for suicide

How to Help Yourself or Others:

Seeking help from a psychologist or psychiatrist is a sign of strength and self-care. Modern therapy is effective in 80-90% of cases of depression and other disorders causing suicidal thoughts.

Emergency Help:

  • Crisis line: 988 (USA, 24/7)
  • Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 1-800-273-TALK
  • World Health Organization: suicide prevention resources
  • In case of acute threat - call emergency: 911
